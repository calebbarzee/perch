Perch extension settings

On product pages, the extension reads the product number (GTIN) from the page. It asks the nodes below for records in a bucket of about 1 in 65,536 products. It never sends the full product number, the page address, or anything else.

The extension checks every signature and computes ratings and prices in your browser with the policy bundled in this extension. A node can withhold records but cannot change them. Use at least 2 independent nodes so the panel can show when they disagree.

One HTTP address per line, for example http://127.0.0.1:7700

The panel links here. Empty: the first node's address, which serves the app.
