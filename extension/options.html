Perch extension settings

On a product page, Perch reads the product number (GTIN) from the page. It asks the nodes below for the records of a group of products and checks every signature in your browser.

What leaves your browser

DataSent toWhen
The first 4 hex characters of sha256(product key)Each node in the list belowOnce per product page with a GTIN, when Perch is shown

Nothing else leaves the browser: no page address, no full product key, no cookies, no Referer header.

A 4-character prefix splits products into 65,536 groups. On a node with 100,000 products, a group holds about 1.5 products, so the node can often tell which product you viewed. On a node with 1,000,000 products, a group holds about 15.

Show Perch

The shop's page is not changed. Click the Perch button or press Alt+Shift+P.

The shop's page can detect the added tab and learn that you use Perch. It cannot read what the tab shows.
